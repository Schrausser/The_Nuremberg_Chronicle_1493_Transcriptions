## Differences between the ENHG versions of 1493 and 1496, Bl. 1v to 7r.

- **Bl. 1v  vs. 1r**: ***Beda*** was *omitted* in the enumeration of important authors.
- **Bl. 5v vs. 5r**: ***nitteilheftig*** vs. *teilheftig* are contradictory statements.
- **Bl. 6v vs. 6r**: *zeittigkeit* [...] *bis* ***xxxviiij***, ***39***  vs.  *xxxviij*, *38*.

- Throughout the whole text, ***welt*** is used consistently instead of *werlt*.

- Newly introduced are the letters ***ä*** and (partly) ***ii*** instead of *ü* (*ue*) or *u*, as well as the abbreviations ***dz*** for *d[as]* and ***wz*** for *w[as]*, in addition *y*, *ey* and *ß* are used more frequently.

- In general, *fewer abbreviations* were made, otherwise it is the same text, with the same sentence position, only a few words were *left out* or *added*.
